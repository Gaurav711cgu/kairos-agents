## Three résumé bullets

- Built a Next.js/FastAPI repository-investigation workbench with PostgreSQL durable command receipts, OIDC tenant authorization, fenced workers and sandboxed patch verification.
- Measured 435,270 local control-plane requests over 42 minutes with zero unexpected errors and 275 ms acknowledgement p95 during a ten-client soak; retained failed runs and verified crash recovery without redispatch.
- Delivered reproducible, inspectable repair evidence and reproduced a recorded Django patch against 110 tests with one skip; diagnosed a context-retention defect and published complete comparisons, including unsuccessful repair studies.
